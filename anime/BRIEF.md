# ほしまいご (Hoshi-maigo, "The Lost Little Star") — production brief

A 60-second, fully procedural 2D animated short. **Every pixel, voice, note and
sound effect is generated by code** in this directory — no downloaded art,
music or samples. 1920×1080, 30 fps.

## Story (see story/timeline.json for exact timings — it is the source of truth)
Once every hundred years a star gets lost. On a summer night a girl, **Hina**,
sees a shooting star crash into the rice field below her village. It is **Kira**,
a tiny star-creature who is crying: it lacks the light to go home. Light made
from people's "thank-you"s can lift it. Hina runs through the town with Kira in
her paper lantern; every house gives a little orb of warm light. Back on the
hill Kira, now grown and radiant, asks her wish: "Will we meet again?" —
"I promise. Every year, on this night, I'll shine on you." Kira rockets up,
bursts into a meteor shower, and the stars settle into a constellation of the
two of them holding hands.

## Art bible
- Look: lush Japanese anime background art (Ghibli / Shinkai feel) done in flat
  vector shapes + soft gradients + additive glows. Layered parallax depth, atmospheric
  perspective (far layers bluer & lighter), subtle rim-light on silhouettes.
- Palette: sky `#050a24 → #132a5c → #2b4a7a`; mountains `#1b2f5e`, `#243d70`, `#30508a`
  (far → lighter); foliage/grass shadows `#0f2a3a`, `#17394a`; warm lights `#ffb347`,
  `#ffd28a`; star/Kira gold `#ffd76a`, `#fff3c0`; Hina yukata navy `#24346e`, obi red `#d8394a`.
- Characters get thin dark outlines (`#1a1433`, ~3px at scale 1); backgrounds have no outlines.
- The village: small valley village, tiled & thatched roofs, terraced rice paddies
  that mirror the stars, a little shrine torii, telephone poles, a hill with one
  huge old camphor tree + a small stone lantern (tōrō) overlooking the valley (the
  "hilltop" in s2 and s6).
- Motion: everything breathes — grass sway, fireflies, twinkling, hair flutter.
  Use easing (engine.js `Ease`, `prog`) and overlap/follow-through. Camera moves
  should be slow and eased (applyCamera).

## Technical rules (IMPORTANT)
- Rendering is HTML5 canvas 2D in headless Chromium (render/index.html).
- **Pure functions of time**: `draw(ctx, t, T, d)` gets local time t, global T, scene
  duration d. Frames are rendered out of order by parallel workers, so NEVER keep
  state between frames, never use Math.random() — use `rng(seed)` (create a fresh
  one inside draw), `hash`, `noise1`, `noise2`, `fbm1` from engine.js.
- Helpers in render/engine.js (read it!): W, H, clamp, lerp, smooth, prog, Ease, rng,
  noise, glow, sparkle, starfield, fireflies, mouthAt, blinkAt, applyCamera, rgba, mixColor.
- Characters: render/characters.js — see API contract at top. Scenes must use
  `drawHina`, `drawKira`, `drawVillager` (the character designer is improving these
  in place; the API stays fixed). Lip-sync: pass `mouth: mouthAt('hina', T)`.
- Subtitles, vignette, grain, scene transitions are added by render/main.js — do not draw subtitles.
  Keep important action out of the bottom ~150px (subtitle area).
- Scene files: render/scenes/sN.js calling `registerScene('sN', { draw(ctx, t, T, d) {...} })`.
  You may add helper functions, but prefix them with your scene id (e.g. `s3_drawCrater`)
  because all scripts share one global scope.
- Performance: aim for < 150 ms per frame. Pre-render nothing across frames; but you MAY
  cache static layers (e.g. a mountain silhouette canvas) in a module-level object keyed
  by parameters, as long as the result is identical regardless of which frame came first.
- Preview: `cd render && node render.mjs still 15.0 18.5 --out /some/dir --scale 0.5`
  then look at the PNGs. Short clip: `node render.mjs video --from 13.5 --to 24 --workers 2 --scale 0.5 --out /some/dir/clip.mp4`.
  Page errors are printed — fix them all.
- Only edit the files you own. Do not git commit; the director integrates.
